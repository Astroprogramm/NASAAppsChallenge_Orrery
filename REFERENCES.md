# 📚 Project References and Sources

This document outlines the scientific data sources, developer documentation, and design assets utilized in the creation of the Cosmic Canvas dashboard.

## 🌌 Scientific Data & Astrophysics

### Major Planets and Sun Facts
* **NASA Science - Sun Facts**: Baseline data for solar physics and solar system mass.  
  👉 [Link to Source](https://science.nasa.gov/sun/facts/)
* **NASA Space Science Data Coordinated Archive (NSSDC)**: Planetary Fact Sheets containing precise mass, sizes, and orbital metrics.  
  👉 [Link to Source](https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/)
* **NASA Solar System Exploration Database**: General composition metrics used for planet descriptions.  
  👉 [Link to Source](https://solarsystem.nasa.gov)

### Trans-Neptunian & Kuiper Belt Objects
* **IAU Minor Planet Center (MPC)**: Reference logs for dwarf planets (Pluto, Quaoar, Haumea, Makemake).  
  👉 [Link to Source](https://minorplanetcenter.net)
* **Caltech Division of Geological and Planetary Sciences**: Research on rotation dynamics and surface ice compositions for outer planetoids.

### Ephemerides and Orbit Calculations
* **Astroquery (JPL Horizons Modules)**: Engine documentation for real-time ephemeris calculations and planetary orbit path arrays.  
  👉 [Link to Source](https://astroquery.readthedocs.io/en/latest/jplhorizons/jplhorizons.html#overview)
